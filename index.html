Prototype. Perk data is hand-curated across 22 airports and 32 cards, and reflects public sources as of September 2026. Airport benefits change constantly — lounges leave networks, restaurants drop out, terminals get reassigned. Confirm in your issuer's app before you count on anything here.

Open now is calculated in the airport's own timezone, not your phone's. Venues with irregular hours show their schedule without a badge rather than risk a wrong one, and anything shut drops to the bottom of its terminal.

Getting there. When you set a terminal, every other terminal is marked with whether you can walk to it without clearing security again. Where a connection is uncertain we err toward telling you to re-screen — a wasted walk costs you minutes, a missed connection costs you the flight.

Military access is compiled from USO's published admissions policy and airline baggage pages, not from lived experience — verify with the USO app before relying on it. The eligibility line that catches people out: active duty, Reserve, Guard, retirees and dependents with a DoD ID all qualify, but veterans without one do not. Baggage rules also differ sharply between travelling on orders and travelling personally.

Guest rules changed a lot in 2026. Capital One ended free Priority Pass guests, Amex cut Centurion to one guest with a $50 fee beyond that, and Sapphire Reserve quietly became the most generous of the three at two guests free.

Why the same "Priority Pass" differs by card: Amex- and Capital One-issued Priority Pass memberships cover lounges only. Chase-issued memberships add non-lounge experiences like Gameway, spas and Minute Suites, but dropped restaurant credits. Citi, Bank of America and U.S. Bank memberships still include restaurant credits. This app models each separately.
